# fal.aiへの確認メール（送付用）

> [[07-fal.ai規約調査ログ]]で残った要確認事項をfal.aiサポートに書面で問い合わせるための下書き。送付後、回答内容をこのファイルに追記し、証跡として保存する。

## 送付先
support@fal.ai

## 件名
Questions regarding MiniMax H3 usage terms for a commercial project

## 本文（英語・送付用）

```
Hello fal team,

We are planning to use MiniMax H3 (and/or H3 Max) via the fal API for a commercial video production project for an enterprise client in Japan. Before proceeding, we would like written confirmation on a few points related to our client's data governance requirements.

1. Model designation (training use)
Per the API Services Terms of Service (Section "Use of Customer Content"), Customer Content is not used to create, train, or develop fal's products or services, except for models marked "Enterprise Pending" (Excluded Models).
- Is MiniMax H3 and/or H3 Max currently designated as an Excluded Model / "Enterprise Pending"?
- If so, does that mean our prompts, input images, and outputs sent to this model could be used for training or model development by fal or MiniMax?

2. Ownership and commercial use of Output Content
The general Terms of Service (Section on AI Features) states that fal does not represent or warrant that Output Content grants any intellectual property rights to the customer.
- Can you confirm in writing that we, as the paying customer, retain the right to commercially use, distribute, and deliver Output Content generated via the API to our own end client, without restriction from fal?

3. IP indemnification
We note that the indemnification clause in the Terms of Service is one-directional (customer indemnifies fal), with no indemnification from fal to the customer regarding third-party IP claims on Output Content.
- Is there any indemnification option available (e.g., under a Business/Enterprise plan) that would protect us against third-party IP claims related to Output Content generated using MiniMax H3?
- We are aware of pending litigation (Disney/Universal/WBD vs. Hailuo/MiniMax regarding training data) and want to properly disclose any related risk to our end client.

4. Account setup
We intend to use a standard paid (pay-as-you-go) account for this project rather than a negotiated Enterprise contract, given the project's modest scale. Please let us know if any of the above assurances require an Enterprise-tier agreement rather than being available on our standard account.

Thank you in advance for your help — written confirmation on these points would let us proceed with confidence and properly document our compliance process for our client.

Best regards,
[お名前]
[会社名/個人事業の名称]
[登録メールアドレス]
```

## 確認している要点（社内記録用・日本語）
1. MiniMax H3（またはH3 Max）が fal.ai上で「除外モデル（エンタープライズ対応保留中）」に指定されていないか
2. 生成物（Output Content）の商用利用権が顧客に帰属することの書面確認
3. 生成物に関する第三者IP侵害クレームへの補償（IP indemnification）の有無（Hailuo訴訟リスクを念頭に）
4. 上記が通常の有償プラン（Pay-as-you-go）で得られるか、Enterprise契約が必要か
